PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

QuranFlow - Beyond Ramadan

1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Product Name
QuranFlow

Tagline
A behavioral and educational system for lifelong Quran engagement.

Vision
To transform Quran engagement from a seasonal activity during Ramadan into a consistent, identity-driven daily practice.

Mission
To build a system that combines behavioral science, Islamic scholarship, AI-driven personalization, and accessibility to help users sustain a long-term, meaningful relationship with the Quran.

2. PROBLEM DEFINITION
Observed Behavior
During Ramadan, users significantly increase Quran engagement due to structure, community, and spiritual motivation. However, immediately after Ramadan:
Engagement drops sharply.
Users lose structure and routine.
Motivation declines without reinforcement.

Key Pain Points
Behavioral.
No structured habit system.
Users rely on motivation instead of systems.
Guilt after inconsistency leads to abandonment.

Educational
Limited understanding of what is being read.
Weak connection between Quran and daily life.

Accessibility
Non-Arabic speakers struggle with comprehension.
Lack of localized explanations (e.g., Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa).

Social
No accountability systems
Lack of supportive community or partners

Root Cause
The issue is not access to the Quran.

It is:
Lack of personalization
Lack of behavioral systems
Lack of identity formation
Lack of guided understanding

Result
Users develop a seasonal relationship with the Quran instead of a sustained, lifelong connection.

3. PRODUCT GOALS
Primary Goal (North Star Metric)
Percentage of users maintaining Quran engagement 60–90 days after Ramadan

Supporting Goals
Increase daily active users (DAU).
Improve weekly consistency.
Increase depth of engagement (reflections, understanding).
Improve retention (7-day, 30-day, 90-day).

5. CORE PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE
Systems Overview
QuranFlow consists of seven interconnected systems:
1. Onboarding & Profiling System
2. Quran Reading Engine
3. Education Engine
4. Habit & Behavior Engine
5. Social & Accountability System
6. AI Personalization Engine
7. Trust, Security & Infrastructure Layer

Each system feeds into the others to create a continuous engagement loop.
